# Seat Belts
All drivers and passengers must wear seat belts at all times while the vehicle is moving.
A driver must not start the vehicle until the seat belt is fastened.

# Mobile Phones
Do not use mobile phones while driving.
If you must make or take a call, park the vehicle safely first.
Do not text, browse or use navigation apps by hand while the vehicle is in motion.

# Vehicle Inspection
Complete a vehicle inspection before departure every day.
Check the tyres, brakes, lights, mirrors, horn, fuel level and engine oil during the inspection.
Do not start a delivery if the vehicle fails the inspection.

# Speed Limits
Follow the posted speed limits at all times.
Reduce speed in bad weather, heavy traffic, school zones and residential areas.
Keep a safe distance from the vehicle in front.

# Reporting Vehicle Problems
Report any vehicle problem to the fleet supervisor immediately.
Do not drive a vehicle with faulty brakes, steering or lights until it is repaired.

# Accident Procedure
In case of an accident, stop the vehicle immediately and switch on the hazard lights.
Check yourself and others for injuries and call emergency services if anyone is hurt.
Inform the fleet supervisor as soon as possible after an accident.
Do not admit fault or move the vehicle unless it blocks traffic, and take photos of the scene.

# Vehicle Breakdown Procedure
If the vehicle breaks down, move it to a safe place away from traffic if possible.
Switch on the hazard lights and place a warning triangle behind the vehicle.
Call the fleet supervisor or roadside assistance and stay in a safe place until help arrives.

# Alcohol and Drugs
Do not drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
Drivers must not consume alcohol or drugs before or during their shift.
Report to your supervisor if you are on medication that causes drowsiness.

# Rest and Fatigue
Take a rest break if you feel tired or sleepy while driving.
